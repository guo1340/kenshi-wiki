Mods

Home / Mods

Kenshi has one of the strongest modding communities in survival gaming. These guides cover the mods wanderers reach for — and the load-order traps to avoid.

Essential Mods

The mods most veterans install before they even start a new game. Quality-of-life so basic it feels like patches.

QoL Mods

Quality-of-life mods that remove tedium without touching difficulty or balance.

Graphics Mods

Visual mods that sharpen Kenshi's sand-worn world — at a performance cost worth measuring.

Combat Mods

Mods that retune fighting — animations, attack slots, damage and AI — for a sharper, fairer brawl.

Overhaul Mods

Total conversions that rebuild factions, items, regions and balance. A second Kenshi inside Kenshi.

Base Building Mods

Mods that expand and smooth base construction — more buildings, better placement, deeper production.

Performance Mods

Mods that fight Kenshi's stutter — texture compression, optimisation tweaks and memory fixes.

Difficulty Mods

Mods for players who found vanilla Kenshi too soft — harsher economies, deadlier enemies, scarcer resources.

Reactive World

A landmark mod that lets your actions reshape towns, factions and the map state.

Genesis

One of the largest Kenshi overhauls — sweeping new factions, items, regions and depth.

Kaizo

An overhaul focused on a coherent, expanded and more challenging wasteland.

Universal Wasteland Expansion

UWE — an overhaul that sharpens Kenshi's survival edge with deeper systems and a harsher economy.

Dark UI

The near-universal interface mod — a darker, cleaner, more readable Kenshi UI.
