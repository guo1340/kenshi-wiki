# 03-kenshi-wiki.md

# Kenshi Wiki Expansion Blueprint

## Project Objective

Transform the Kenshi Wiki from a collection of game information into a practical player strategy resource.

Google already understands:

- item names
- weapon stats
- faction names
- location descriptions

Our content should focus on:

- decision making
- survival strategy
- progression planning
- player mistakes
- faction choices
- base building tradeoffs

The site should feel like it was written by players with hundreds of hours in Kenshi.

---

# Site Positioning

Primary Audience:

- New players
- Returning players
- Players overwhelmed by Kenshi's complexity

Primary Promise:

"Learn how to survive and thrive in Kenshi without wasting dozens of hours making avoidable mistakes."

---

# Content Generation Philosophy

Do NOT generate pages that simply repeat game facts.

Bad Example:

"The Shek Kingdom is a major faction located in western Kenshi."

Good Example:

"The Shek Kingdom is one of the easiest factions for combat-focused players to align with early, but their aggressive culture can create challenges for players attempting trade-focused or stealth-oriented runs."

Every page should answer:

- Why does this matter?
- Should I care?
- Is this worth doing?
- What do experienced players recommend?
