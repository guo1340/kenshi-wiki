# Kenshi Wiki — Content Blueprint & Design System

## Project Goal
Create a high-quality, SEO-focused, community-driven Kenshi wiki similar in structure and usability to the existing Project Zomboid wiki, but adapted specifically to Kenshi’s atmosphere, mechanics, factions, worldbuilding, and survival gameplay loop.

The wiki should feel:
- Harsh
- Desolate
- Post-apocalyptic
- Sand-worn
- Ancient-tech mixed with scavenger survival
- Minimal but immersive

Tone should sound like:
- A veteran wanderer documenting survival knowledge
- Practical, experience-based advice
- Opinionated where useful
- Beginner-friendly while still deep enough for advanced players

The wiki should avoid sounding like:
- Generic AI summaries
- Dry encyclopedia text
- Overly corporate writing
- Overly meme-heavy content

Instead:
- Explain WHY something matters
- Mention beginner traps
- Mention long-term consequences
- Compare risk vs reward
- Explain faction relationships and survival implications
