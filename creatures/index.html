Creatures

Home / Creatures

The wasteland is full of things that hunt. These guides rate every creature by danger, behaviour, loot and how to counter — or avoid — it.

Beak Things Extreme

The wasteland's apex predator — fast, huge and merciless. The creature that teaches new players to check the horizon.

Leviathans High

Colossal creatures of the Leviathan Coast — slow, peaceful, and catastrophic if you pick a fight.

Bonedogs Low

Loyal, hardy wasteland dogs — manageable as a threat and excellent as squad members.

Blood Spiders High

Fast pack hunters of the swamp — dangerous in numbers, and a hard lesson for unprepared travellers.

Gorillos High

Powerful ape-like creatures that hit hard up close. Dangerous individually, not just in numbers.

River Raptors Moderate

Fast reptilian predators of wetlands and coasts — quick, persistent, and dangerous to stragglers.

Security Spiders High

Ancient robotic guardians that still patrol ruins, executing security protocols for an empire long gone.

Fogmen Extreme

Feral Hivers of the Fog Islands who attack in endless waves and devour the downed. A creature-threat as much as a faction.
